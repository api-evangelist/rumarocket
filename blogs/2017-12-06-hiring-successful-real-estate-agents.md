There are already a lot of information available about what things to consider when hiring real estate agents. For most real estate companies, they look at factors such as experience, knowledge about the industry, educational background or personal network to gauge if a candidate is likely to be productive. However, this kind of hiring strategy has always been a hit-and-miss.
