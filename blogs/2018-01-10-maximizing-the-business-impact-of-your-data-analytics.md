Is your data only giving you one story of how your business is run? In an economy that has become increasingly data-driven, the biggest question on most companies minds still is: How can I use the data I have to make more effective decisions in my organisation? Data has become an extremely valuable resource, used as a unit of experience for companies to derive better decisions and solutions to their existing challenges.
