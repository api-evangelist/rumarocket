Don’t wait until your employees start taking hostages to start actively listening to them….it just might cost you your job. Our entire office watched riveted by the hostage situation that was unfolding before our eyes. An ex-guard had stormed his former workplace in Manila-armed with a gun and grenade-and taken 30 of his former co-workers hostage.
